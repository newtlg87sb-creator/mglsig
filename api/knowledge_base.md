## Subscription plans

The pricing page currently displays these plans:

- Free: $0. Includes basic signals, news, projects, and the knowledge section; Alpha signals are locked.
- Alpha: $20 per month. Includes all signals, news/projects, knowledge resources, Alpha access, and exclusive features.
- Beta: $10 per month. Includes the interactive Iceberg signal and basic signals, news, projects, and knowledge resources; Alpha signals are excluded.
- Gamma: $10 per month. Provides access to the paid signals on `paidsignal2.html`.
- Delta and Epsilon are shown as inactive.

The paid plans are presented as monthly access. These prices and plan contents are not guaranteed and may change; `main_pricing.html` is the current reference. Do not infer renewal, cancellation, refund, or payment status.

- Never ask users to share passwords, API keys, wallet seed phrases, payment credentials, or other sensitive information in chat.
